About the project

A filtration mask that also tracks how you're doing

The mask filters the air you breathe with two blower fans and a replaceable filter. Its sensors watch your heart, the air inside the mask and the state of the filter, then send it all to this app over Bluetooth.

How the scores work

Recovery (0–100%). Compares this morning's resting heart rate and HRV with your own 14-day baseline. Lower resting HR and higher HRV than usual push the score up. Sleep counts too when sleep tracking is on.

Strain (0–21). Adds up time in each heart-rate zone, weighting harder zones more. The scale is logarithmic, so going from 18 to 19 takes far more effort than 8 to 9.

Air quality score (0–100). Combines the outdoor AQI where you are (60%) with CO₂ inside the mask (40%). 100 means clean air and fresh airflow.

What each tab is for

  • Today: the three scores and live readings.
  • Recovery, Strain, Sleep: heart data in detail.
  • Air: AQI, CO₂, airflow and filter.
  • Trends: weekly and monthly history plus an auto summary.
  • Journal: tag sessions like "football practice" to compare activities.
  • Alerts: reseal, filter, hazardous AQI and battery warnings.
  • Device: pairing, sensors and the Bluetooth data format.

This is a student/prototype project, not a medical device. Don't use its readings to make health decisions.
